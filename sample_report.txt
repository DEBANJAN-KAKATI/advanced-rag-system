# Q3 Financial Performance Report

Revenue for Q3 reached $4.2 million, representing a 24% year-over-year increase driven by strong enterprise subscription growth.
Operating expenses totaled $2.1 million, resulting in a net profit margin of 50%.

Customer retention remains high at 96.5% gross dollar retention.
